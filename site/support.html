Support & FAQ

Frequently asked questions and support for Smack-a-Pad for iPad.

Which controllers work with Smack-a-Pad?

Smack-a-Pad works with class-compliant USB and Bluetooth MIDI pad controllers, on-screen pads, and a hardware keyboard.

How do I restore purchases?

If you reinstalled the app or set up a new iPad, you can restore your purchases at any time:

  1. Open Smack-a-Pad and tap the Settings icon (gear).
  2. Go to the Purchases section (or open any lesson purchase prompt).
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

StoreKit will verify your entitlements with Apple and unlock your content without charging you again.

How can I back up my progress?

Your practice history, mastered lessons, and settings are saved locally on your device in a file named progress.json.

  • Files App: Smack-a-Pad has file sharing enabled. You can find and copy your progress.json file at any time in the iPad Files app under On My iPad → Smack-a-Pad. You can also back it up to a Mac using Finder.
  • iCloud Device Backup: If you have iPadOS iCloud Backup enabled for your iPad, app data is automatically preserved when backing up your tablet.

What does Full Unlock include?

Full Unlock is a single one-time purchase with no subscription. It unlocks the entire base library of lessons, tracks, and future base content permanently. Free introductory lessons and exercises remain free forever.
