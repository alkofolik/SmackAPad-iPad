# App Review notes

No MIDI controller or login is needed to review Smack-a-Pad.

## Play with the on-screen pads

1. Hold the iPad in portrait orientation.
2. On the home screen, open **Controller** and select **16 pads, notes 36–51** under **Controller profiles**.
3. If the touch pads were turned off, enable **Touch pads in portrait** under **Settings → Practice screen**.
4. Return to the home screen, tap **Intro**, and open an exercise. Tap the pads at the bottom of the lesson to play sounds. Tap **Start** to play along with the exercise.

## Play with a hardware keyboard

1. Connect a hardware keyboard to the iPad.
2. Select **16 pads, notes 36–51** under **Controller → Controller profiles**.
3. In **Settings → Keyboard**, make sure **Play pads with keyboard** is enabled.
4. Open an **Intro** exercise. Press the keys in the four rows shown in **Settings → Keyboard** to play the pads. On a US-layout keyboard the rows are **1 2 3 4**, **Q W E R**, **A S D F**, and **Z X C V**. Tap **Start** to play along.

## Purchases and Bluetooth

Intro and Path stage 1 are free. The non-consumable purchase `cz.kju.SmackAPad.full` unlocks the remaining content. **Restore Purchases** is available in **Settings → Purchases** and in the purchase sheet.

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect MIDI controllers.
