### Description (1665 / 4000 chars)

Smack-a-Pad teaches you to play drum patterns on pads: on a MIDI controller, on the iPad's touch pads or on a keyboard. Short lessons, honest timing feedback and a training plan that brings back what you are about to forget.

Start with the free Intro and the first stage of the Path, then unlock the full course when you are ready.

HOW YOU PRACTISE
• 587 lessons, from slow starter beats to fills, ghost notes, swing, electronic and world rhythms (58 of them free)
• Every lesson is a pattern on a grid, so you see which pad to hit and when
• Play along with the metronome; the tempo rises as your playing gets cleaner
• Earn up to three stars per lesson. The third star comes only when you can still play it from memory on separate days
• Training brings back lessons that are due for review, so what you learn stays learned
• Mix gives you a varied round of lessons you already know
• Browse the Library by path, genre or technique

BUILT FOR PADS
• Works with MIDI pad controllers over USB or Bluetooth, with ready-made controller profiles and your own through MIDI learn
• No controller? Play on the on-screen pads (iPad in portrait) or on a hardware keyboard
• Guided calibration measures your device's latency
• Eight sound kits: acoustic, 808, jazz, rock, brush, house, world and world percussion
• Volume for each instrument, so a loud crash never drowns the rest

YOUR DATA
• No account needed and no data collected
• Your progress stays on your device; export it to a file and import it back any time
• English and Czech, light and dark mode

Free to download. Intro and the first stage of the Path are free; one in-app purchase unlocks everything else.
