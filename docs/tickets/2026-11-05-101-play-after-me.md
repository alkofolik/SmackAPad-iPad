# 101 — Play after me (call and response)

Status: ready-for-human. Depends on: 98 (Training screen card). Source: Jakub, 2026-11-05 (learning by ear and feel, without reading).

## Goal

The app plays a phrase, the player plays it back, without notes. This trains the ear and the hands to work without the pattern on screen. It works for any lesson in Practice and as a card on the Training screen.

## Rules

1. New practice mode next to Listen and Play: **"After me"** / **"Po mně"** (same switch control, same look).
2. One cycle = **call** then **response**, each one lesson length (`lesson.bars`):
   - Call: the lesson sound plays the pattern with the metronome; the player's hits sound but are not scored.
   - Response: the metronome continues, no lesson sound; the player's hits are scored against the same pattern.
   - Cycles run continuously without gaps until STOP. One count-in only at START.
3. Notes are **hidden** in both halves (grid skeleton, counts and playhead stay). A clear label above the pattern: "Listen…" / "Poslouchej…" during the call, "Your turn" / "Teď ty" during the response.
4. After each response the bar verdicts appear on its bars as in Play and clear at the next call.
5. Tempo: starts at the current lesson tempo; no automatic tempo change; the tempo control works when stopped.
6. **No stars, no Learning credit, no reviews.** It is practice only; the result card shows the last and the best response score and says "Practice only · no stars" / "Jen cvičení · bez hvězd".
7. Section loop: a selected section is used as the phrase (call and response are the section length).
8. Training screen card **Play after me** / **Zahraj po mně**: picks one lesson with star 1 or more (prefer lessons without star 3), opens it in After me mode. "Another lesson" / "Jiná lekce" button.
