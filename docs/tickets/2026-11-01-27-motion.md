## Goal

Add decent, modern, short animations to the chrome of the app, exactly the 15 items of the proposal. Read that document first; it is the specification (principles, table, cost and risk). This ticket adds only the technical rules.

## Rules

1. **One token set** in `Theme.swift`: `Theme.Motion.fast` (0.15 s ease-out), `.standard` (0.25 s ease-out), `.emphasis` (spring, response 0.4, damping 0.8), `.rise` (6 pt offset used with fades). Every animation uses these; no ad-hoc durations (the existing `StarBurst` and `TempoUpBanner` timings may be moved onto the tokens where it changes nothing visible).
2. **Reduce Motion** (`accessibilityReduceMotion`): one helper (for example `View.motion(_:value:)` or a `Theme.Motion.animation(reduce:)` function) that returns an opacity-only change of at most 0.2 s, never movement, scale, springs or pulses. Use it everywhere; do not scatter `if reduceMotion` checks.
3. **Never touch timing-critical drawing**: the Canvas pattern, the position marker, hit judging, scoring, audio scheduling and `TimelineView` redraws stay as they are. Item 11 (hit colours) is allowed only as colour interpolation of the already computed mark colour inside the existing drawing code, and must be dropped if it costs frames. No animation may be driven from the audio or scoring path.
4. **Deployment target is iOS 17.0.** The zoom navigation transition (item 1/2) needs iOS 18: use it behind `#available(iOS 18, *)` and fall back to fade + scale 0.98 (`.fast`/`.standard`). Do not raise the deployment target.
5. Use the shared components from ticket 25A (`Components.swift`: `ListRow`, `StarsView`, `ProgressLine`, `FilterChip`, `InfoBanner`, ...) as the single place for the animations of items 5-8, so Library and Intro move alike.
6. Colours: orange, black, white and the existing result shades only. No haptics, no sound.
7. Texts: no new user-facing text expected; if any appears, both `en` and `cs` catalogs.

## Items (see the proposal for details)

1-2 Library <-> Practice zoom with fallback. 3 Cross-fade when switching Path/Genres/Techniques and groups. 4 Level/stage disclosure with chevron rotation. 5 New star pops once in the library/Intro row after returning from practice (compare stars before and after the practice screen, animate only the new ones). 6 Progress lines animate on appear and change. 7 Filter chips fade, counts use numeric text transition. 8 Unlock banner: "Unlock" -> "Really?" cross-fade in place; after unlocking the banner collapses and lock icons turn open then fade. 9 START/STOP buttons morph in place. 10 Count-in numerals scale 1.15 -> 1.0 while fading in on each beat (pattern stays dimmed). 11 Hit colour transition about 0.12 s; the trailing tail from ticket 24 fades 0.25 s (reuse what 24 built, do not duplicate). 12 Tempo number rolls (numeric text transition) plus the existing pulse. 13 Star earned: staggered star fill (80 ms apart) and a soft fade of the pattern behind the burst. 14 End-of-run strip slides up with `.emphasis`, stars fill in sequence. 15 Sheets and popovers keep system motion; only inner toggles/rows use `.fast`.
