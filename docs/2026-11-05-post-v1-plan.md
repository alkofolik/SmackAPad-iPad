# Plan after v1.0

Source: Jakub, 2026-11-05. Ideas agreed in principle but **not in v1.0**. No tickets yet; each becomes a ticket when 1.0 is out. Related v1.0 work: tickets 97–101 (Training).

## 1. Songs built from lessons

- Longer pieces assembled from existing lessons, as in the legacy tool (phase 5 "Skladby", 2026-10-04: 13 songs). Each song has sections (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, breakdown, build, groove, drop, outro); a section is a list of `{lesson, repeat, variant}` parts; variant `no-kick` drops the kick pads.
- Two versions per song: Short (16 bars) and Full.
- Played as a "virtual lesson": hits assembled from lesson data at load time and fed to the existing player, scoring and section loop (same approach as the ticket 59 repeated run). Lessons themselves never change.
- Notation for long pieces: pages of 4 bars or scrolling, with a thin overview strip of sections (SPEC already plans this).
- Open: the legacy catalogue file (`2026-10-04-skladby-katalog.json`) was not found in the legacy folder on 2026-11-05; locate it before the ticket. Song progress storage (separate record keyed `song-…`).

## 2. Chaining (learning a long piece part by part)

- Learn part A, then B, then A+B, then C, then B+C, then A+B+C. The loop grows by itself once the current chain is clean at the chain's tempo.
- Applies to songs and to any section selection in the section loop.

## 3. Groove + break phrases

- A 4-bar phrase from two lessons the player already has: 3 bars of a groove lesson + 1 bar of a fills lesson (8-bar variant: 7 + 1). No new lesson data needed.
- The natural musical step from 1–3-bar patterns to real phrases.

## 4. Results by part

- After a long run, the result shows a score per section or bar group and names the weakest one.
- Button "Practise this place" / "Procvičit toto místo": starts a section loop on the weakest bar or section, about 10 BPM slower (legacy diagnosis, concept 2026-10-06 §6.2).
- A gentle "slow down" suggestion after two weak runs in a row (< 60 %). Suggestion only; automatic tempo rise stays as it is.
