# Virtuosity selection for Pad-O-Fill

Sources: Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples; drummer Austin McMahon; Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston. CC0 1.0.
https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

A selected web palette, not the complete upstream library or its microphone mixer. Five representative drum layers, original velocity splits on cymbals and percussion, four actual round robins for closed hat/ride/crash, three for open hat, two for auxiliary percussion. Shaker has one captured velocity layer. PCM conversion and very quiet edge trimming are recorded for every output in ../provenance.json. No loudness normalization or invented round robins. Jazz's clap remains the AVL sample licensed CC BY-SA 3.0; the World palette combines existing AVL drums with Virtuosity shaker, low woodblock and cowbell. No snare or tom is relabelled as a conga or bongo.
