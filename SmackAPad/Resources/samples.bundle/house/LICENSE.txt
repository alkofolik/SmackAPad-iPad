# House/Techno · synthesized

Original sounds synthesized by tools/build_synth.py (sine sweeps, filtered noise, square-wave metals). No recorded samples.

License of the files in this folder: CC0 1.0 (original synthesis by the Smack-a-Pad project; no third-party samples).

## Changes made

Mixed multiple microphones to mono (where the source had several), trimmed leading silence (1 ms kept), shortened and faded the tail, converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV, selected up to 5 velocity layers and 1–2 round-robin takes, and matched the level of the loudest layer to the same instrument in the Acoustic kit (peak limit −1 dBFS). Exact source files and SHA-256 hashes are in `provenance-new.json`.

The sound content is not endorsed by the original authors.
